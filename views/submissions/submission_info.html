Submit your own content and feeedback

This site is largely built with content created by students and is continuously updated with new content.*

Your submissions and contributions help grow and improve the platform, and are greatly appreciated.**

In order to contribute with content, you must be a registered user. This helps ensure the course information submitted is reliable and properly sourced, your profile will therefore also be linked to your submissions.


Students

As a student you can help build and support the site by submitting your own materials and materials from class. This can include but is not limited to study lists, guides, and tips or old worksheeets, previous exams and exam questions (e.g questions you got asked during an oral exam). The submisson form can also be used to report errors or suggested corrections to existing material.


Professors (of the featured courses)

As a professor you can help supplement the content by submitting feedback or materials. This can include but is not limited to reporting errors or issues in existing content, curriculum updates or changes, additional material that would benefit students.



*If you only see sparce or no information about a course, it is most likely due to there not having been any submissions on that course. However, you can write an e-mail or call IT support if in doubt.

**By submitting content, you grant the site owners and administrators permission to use, publish, edit, and distribute your materials on this platform. Submitted content may be edited and may remain published even if you later delete your account.

About Exam Ease

Exam Ease is a platform designed to simplify exam preparation and study management for students across multiple Aalborg University programs and semesters.

By gathering up-to-date exam information, previous exam sets, curriculum overviews detailing the topics covered in the course, and course details, in one convenient place, Exam Ease makes it easier for students to access information they need to know about their courses and exams.

The platform provides students with a starting point for exam preparation. Making it easier for students to start creating effective study plans while the integrated progress tracker allows them to easily monitor their study progress and stay on top of their goals. Helping students feel prepared for their exams, stay organized, and navigate their courses and curriculum stress-free.
